# Draft letters on the South Brunswick data center air permit

**Draft for discussion.** These letters were drafted by a community member for the Princeton Board of Education to consider. They have not been adopted, approved or sent by the Board and do not represent its position.

They concern NJDEP draft air permit PCP250001 for twelve diesel backup generators at 4405 Route 1, South Brunswick, NJ. Public comments are due November 9, 2026.
